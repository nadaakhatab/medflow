What is Medflow?

Medflow is a thyroid-focused medical RAG platform that strictly retrieves information from indexed medical documents before generating responses.

1. Retrieves Evidence

Searches a curated vector database of clinical PDFs to find the most relevant medical evidence for your query.

2. Reranks Sources

3. Generates Citations

Synthesizes a response grounded exclusively in the retrieved text, providing exact page-level citations.

Thyroid Disease Knowledge Base

Peer-reviewed summaries extracted from RAG index documents

Underactive

Hypothyroidism

Characterized by insufficient thyroid hormone production (T3/T4). Leads to metabolic slowdown, fatigue, weight gain, and cold intolerance.

Key Lab Indicator: TSH > 4.5 mIU/L
First Line Rx: Levothyroxine (T4)
Overactive

Hyperthyroidism

Excessive synthesis of thyroid hormones resulting in accelerated metabolism, tachycardia, weight loss, and anxiety.

Key Lab Indicator: TSH < 0.4 mIU/L
First Line Rx: Methimazole / Beta-blockers
Autoimmune

Graves' Disease

Autoimmune disorder causing hyperthyroidism due to thyroid-stimulating immunoglobulins (TSI) binding to TSH receptors.

Antibody Marker: TSI / TRAb positive
Key Feature: Exophthalmos / Goiter
Autoimmune

Hashimoto Thyroiditis

Chronic lymphocytic thyroiditis resulting in progressive autoimmune destruction of thyroid follicular cells.

Antibody Marker: Anti-TPO & Anti-Tg
Complication: Permanent Hypothyroidism
Structural

Thyroid Nodules

Discrete lesions within the thyroid gland distinct from surrounding parenchyma. Evaluated via Ultrasound & TI-RADS.

Evaluation Tool: US + Fine Needle Biopsy
Malignancy Risk: 5% - 15%
Oncology

Thyroid Cancer

Malignancies arising from thyroid follicular or C-cells (Papillary, Follicular, Medullary, Anaplastic variants).

Most Common: Papillary (80-85%)
Main Treatment: Thyroidectomy + RAI

Differential Diagnosis Matrix

Interactive comparison of thyroid etiologies grounded in clinical document chunks

Special Thyroid Clinical States

Specialized monitoring pathways for patients without functioning thyroid tissue or with congenital conditions.

Surgical / Ablated

Post-Thyroidectomy / Athyreotic State

Patients who underwent total thyroidectomy or radioiodine ablation. Evaluates exogenous levothyroxine substitution, malabsorption risks, and cancer TSH suppression targets.

Pediatric / Congenital

Congenital Hypothyroidism

Infants and children born with thyroid agenesis, dysgenesis, or dyshormonogenesis. Uses age-specific pediatric standards and strictly prohibits adult reference ranges.
